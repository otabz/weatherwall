## Open-Meteo weather and geocoding

This app requests current weather and city-search data from Open-Meteo.

- Provider: Open-Meteo, https://open-meteo.com/
- Weather documentation: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs
- Geocoding documentation: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/geocoding-api
- Licence information: https://open-meteo.com/en/licence

The geocoding results are based on GeoNames data, as documented by Open-Meteo.
The app saves only the selected location label, coordinates, location mode and
timezone in Android app preferences.

The app maps Open-Meteo weather codes and measurements into original,
programmatically drawn wallpaper scenes. Open-Meteo does not endorse this
application.

pricing before releasing a paid, advertising-supported or otherwise
